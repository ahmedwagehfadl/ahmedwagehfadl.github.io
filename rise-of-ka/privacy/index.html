Privacy Policy for Rise of Ka: The Last Ascension

Effective date: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the mobile game Rise of Ka: The Last Ascension, referred to in this policy as the “Game.”

1. Information We Collect

The Game does not require users to create an account, log in, or directly provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

The developer does not directly collect or store personal information from users through the Game.

However, the Game uses third-party advertising and mediation services that may automatically collect or process certain information when advertisements are displayed.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The Game uses Unity LevelPlay, formerly known as ironSource LevelPlay, to display and manage advertisements.

Unity LevelPlay and the advertising networks connected through its mediation platform may collect or process information including:

  • Device and advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Approximate location inferred from the IP address
  • Device type, operating system, language, and technical information
  • Advertising interaction data
  • Information about advertisements viewed or clicked
  • Application usage and product interaction data
  • Performance and diagnostic information
  • Information used for fraud prevention, security, advertising measurement, and ad delivery

This information may be used to:

  • Display advertisements
  • Provide personalized or non-personalized advertisements
  • Measure advertising performance
  • Limit how often advertisements are shown
  • Detect fraud, abuse, or invalid advertising activity
  • Improve advertising and mediation services

The collection and use of information by Unity and participating advertising networks are governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.

Users are encouraged to review the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and the privacy policies of any advertising providers that may display advertisements in the Game.

3. Personalized Advertising and Consent

Depending on your country or region, the Game or its advertising providers may request your consent before using information for personalized advertising.

Where required by applicable law, users may be offered the choice to:

  • Accept personalized advertisements
  • Receive non-personalized or contextual advertisements
  • Manage or withdraw their privacy consent
  • Request that personal information not be sold or shared for targeted advertising

The availability of these choices may depend on the user’s location, device, operating system, and the advertising providers used by Unity LevelPlay.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Game is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If the Game is made available to children or included in Google Play’s Families program, additional child-directed advertising settings and restrictions may apply.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information through the Game may contact us using the contact information provided below.

5. Data Sharing

The developer does not directly sell users’ personal information.

Information processed by Unity LevelPlay or participating advertising networks may be shared with advertising partners, measurement providers, attribution providers, service providers, and fraud-prevention providers as necessary to provide advertising services.

Each third-party provider is responsible for its own privacy and data-processing practices.

6. Data Retention

The developer does not maintain a database containing users’ personal information.

Information collected by Unity LevelPlay and participating advertising providers may be retained according to their respective privacy policies, legal obligations, and business requirements.

7. Data Security

Reasonable steps are taken to use reputable advertising and technology providers. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

The security of information processed by third-party advertising providers is governed by their own security practices and privacy policies.

8. User Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent
  • Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, where applicable

Requests relating to information processed by Unity or an advertising network may need to be submitted directly to the relevant provider.

Users may also be able to manage advertising preferences through their device’s privacy or advertising settings.

9. External Links and Advertisements

Advertisements displayed in the Game may contain links to external websites, applications, or services.

The developer is not responsible for the content, security, or privacy practices of external services. Users should review the privacy policy of any third-party service before providing information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the Game, its advertising services, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any updates will be published on the same page, and the effective date shown at the top of the policy will be revised.
